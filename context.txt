## Project: myoptiondashboard.netlify.app

## Repos
- Frontend:  D:\option-dashboard       → Netlify auto-deploy on push
- Backend:   D:\option-dashboard\backend → Render auto-deploy on push
- GSheet writer: D:\optionchain\gsheet_bnf.py → runs locally every 5 min

## Backend (Render) — screener_proxy.py
- Flask proxy serving frontend requests
- BG thread: runs every 5 min, writes ScreenerData to GSheet (20 bull + 20 bear)
- Market hours guard: skips BG cycle outside Mon-Fri 09:15-15:35 IST
- Keep-warm self-ping every 8 min (prevents Render free tier spin-down)
- CORS, fno.csv, Google credentials (env var) all on Render
- ORB Two-Phase System:
  - Phase 1 (once at 9:30am): scans all 209 F&O stocks via yfinance 15min candles
    finds ORB High > PDH (bull) or ORB Low < PDL (bear) → builds _orb_shortlist
  - Phase 2 (every 5min, instant): checks _orb_shortlist vs already-fetched quotes
    trigger = day_high > orb_high (bull) or day_low < orb_low (bear) — candle H/L not LTP
    updates _orb_results cache, writes to GSheet ORBData tab
  - /orb endpoint: serves from _orb_results cache instantly (no yfinance on demand)

## Frontend (Netlify)
- useSheetData.js: fetches BNFData/NFData, rawRows for OIChart, parsedRows for others
- Dashboard.jsx: passes rawRows to OIChart, parsedRows to StraddleStrangle
- IntradayScreener.jsx:
  - On mount: loadFromSheet() fetches last 40 rows of ScreenerData (instant, <1sec)
  - Then immediately: runScreener() for live data + fetchOrb() for ORB tab
  - Every 5 min: auto-refresh from Render
  - Manual Refresh: triggers runScreener() immediately
- OIChart.jsx: strike dropdown per chart (ATM+1/ATM/ATM-1), user-selectable
  data from rawRows filtered by strike (no separate ATM sheet tabs needed)

## GSheet tabs written by gsheet_bnf.py (local)
- BNFData, NFData         — option chain raw rows (11 strikes every 5 min)
- BNiftyPCR, NiftyPCR     — overall CE/PE OI history with composite signal
- Banknifty2, Nifty2      — dashboard display tab
- BNF_Chart, NF_Chart     — embedded charts
- ScreenerData            — top 20 bull + 20 bear every 5 min (written by Render BG thread)
- ORBData                 — ORB setups + status updated every 5 min (written by Render BG thread)

## Signal System (BNiftyPCR)
- 5-factor composite score: ΔOI Flow(40%) + IV Skew(30%) + Max Pain(20%) + OTM(10%)
- Signals: SC (Sell Call), SP (Sell Put), SS (Short Straddle), SG (Short Strangle)
           BC (Buy Call), BP (Buy Put), — (no signal)

## ATR
- Calculated from yfinance daily OHLC — not a direct yfinance value
- Fallback: uses available days (min 2) if < 14 days data
- atr_consumed = abs(ltp - day_open) / atr * 100
- Filter in screener: dropdown ≤50/60/70/80/90% or Off (default 80%)

## ORB Strategy (user-defined)
- Setup: ORB High > Prev Day High (bull) / ORB Low < Prev Day Low (bear)
- Entry: candle HIGH crosses ORB High (bull) / candle LOW crosses ORB Low (bear)
- Stop:  ORB Low (bull) / ORB High (bear)
- Target: ORB High + 1×ATR (reference only — user checks DZ/SZ on 5/15min chart)
- ATR filter: skip if ATR consumed > 80% at entry time
